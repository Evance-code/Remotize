About Remotize

We help Africans learn digital skills, work remotely, and earn global income from anywhere in the world.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: Empower individuals with digital skills and mentorship to succeed in the global remote economy.

Vision: A world where every African can build sustainable online careers and achieve financial freedom.

Our Story

2020

Humble Beginnings

Remotize started as a small WhatsApp group sharing remote job opportunities. Within months, we grew to 500+ members.

2021

First Online Course

Launched our first structured course “Freelancing 101” – 200 students enrolled in the first week.

2022

Community Expansion

Expanded to 5 African countries, partnered with local tech hubs, and introduced mentorship programs.

2024

Milestone: $1M+ Earnings

Our community collectively earned over $1 million through freelancing and remote work.

2026

Today & Beyond

Now serving 10,000+ students, with plans to launch a full remote work academy and job placement platform.
